# product-discovery

Discovery synthesis and validation planning for PMs and BAs: evidence log, problem and outcome, opportunity and solution comparison, assumption test cards, or a research plan when no evidence exists yet. Deliverable: `output/discovery-<slug>.md` with a download link.

## Files

- `skill.md`: the skill. English text; the document and the reply follow the member's language.
- `references/template.md`: document template, read on demand by the skill.
- `references/LICENSE.upstream.txt`: the upstream MIT licence, verbatim.
